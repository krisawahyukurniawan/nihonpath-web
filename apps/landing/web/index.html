Geography & trail sources

The profile uses the brief’s five-point, 7.5 km / 6h 30m reconstruction. Intermediate distances and timings are illustrative—not an official measured dataset. Kengamine is a separate summit destination beyond the Yoshida crater-rim arrival.

Official Mt. Fuji Climbing Guide ↗Geospatial Information Authority of Japan ↗

Language benchmarks

2,136 Jōyō kanji: the 2010 national list. 1,026 Kyōiku kanji: the later elementary-school allocation. N5 (~103) and N4 (~300) are curriculum estimates; JLPT has not published fixed kanji or vocabulary lists since the 2010 revision. Jōyō coverage is not an N1 pass guarantee.

Jōyō Kanji Hyō · Cabinet Notice No. 2 ↗JLPT official FAQ ↗

Estimates, not observations

Temperature = 15.4 − 0.0065 × (elevation − 2,305) °C. This ISA-style lapse estimate uses an assumed summer baseline, not a weather reading. Time, distance and elevation interpolate between the five illustration points. This is a learning metaphor—not navigation, weather or climbing advice.

07 / FROM JAKARTA, WITH INTENTION

Built for the long journey. Not the perfect streak.

NihonPath begins with a simple belief: learning a language should open your world, not overwhelm it. NihonPath Studio Jakarta is shaping a calm, carefully engineered companion for that journey.

We connect structured progression with bilingual understanding and an offline-first vision. This is an independent project in development. The studio below is a working browser experiment—a small piece of what meaningful practice can feel like.
